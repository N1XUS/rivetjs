### Resume fuzz on the derived model (cdk story app, shards 1/3–3/3, `--maxWorkers=1`)

- Build: `RIVET_RESUME_MODEL=derived` passes, with 0 RV2103 and 0 RV2104; the census covers 263 files.
- 64 routes, 1,793 steps driven. 8 routes have no findings; 2 of them drive 0 steps.
- Findings: 24 `recreated`, 100 `mismatch`, 14 `load`, 4 `missing`, 1 `reload`.
- Before the discarded-hole fix the same build had 28 `recreated` and 85 `mismatch`. More steps reach comparison now, because more pages adopt.
- Root causes, pinned in `libs/cdk/tests-fuzz/pins-derived.ts` (all `design`):
  - **Cold press** (`PRESS`): the derived loader holds no pointerdown / pointerup, and dom-resume's cold-press replay is not ported. The first usePress press is lost, so there is no `data-pressed`, focus or open overlay; an overlay's `inert` siblings follow from that.
  - **Adopt mismatch, then browser render** (`RECREATE`): collection hosts (`/autocomplete`, `/select`, `/listbox`, `/tag-group`, `/tree`, …). A `computed` first evaluated inside a probe caches the probe's view. The held event's target leaves the page and the event is dropped.
  - **Mount effects** (`MOUNT`): as in the serialized model's design pins.
